Aftercare

Look after your new ink

How well your tattoo heals decides how it looks for life. Pick a stage to see what to do.

Do

  • Wash your hands before touching the tattoo
  • Wash gently with lukewarm water and mild, fragrance-free soap, then pat dry
  • Wear loose, clean, breathable clothing over it
  • Drink water and get good sleep to help your skin heal

Don't

  • Pick, scratch or peel flakes and scabs
  • Soak in pools, the sea, baths or saunas while healing
  • Put it in direct sun or use tanning beds
  • Use thick layers of ointment, or perfumed lotions

See a doctor if you notice spreading redness, growing swelling, heat, pus, a bad smell or fever. If Erandi gives you different instructions for your tattoo, follow hers.
